Privacy Policy

Effective November 9, 2026

The short version: we collect only what we need to build your career dashboard and, if you ask, to email you pilot updates. We don't sell your information, we don't run ads, and we don't use tracking or analytics tools. You can delete your data at any time.

Who We Are

Ladder Up (ladder-up.net) is a free career roadmap for future and current firefighters, EMTs and paramedics in Illinois, currently in a pilot. Ladder Up is operated by Jack Michael Srebro, 16269 Carlow Circle, Manhattan, IL 60442. Email: jack@ladder-up.net.

What We Collect

When you create an account

  • Your first name, last name and email address.
  • A password. Your password goes straight to our login provider (Netlify) and is never visible to us.
  • If you sign in with Google, Google shares your name and email with us. We don't get access to anything else in your Google account.

When you answer the questionnaire

  • Your career stage, goal, timeline, preferred class schedule, whether you'll need help paying, focus areas and certifications you hold.
  • Your home ZIP code and how far you'll travel. We use the ZIP only to measure distances. We never ask for your street address or track your location.
  • Optional answers: your age range and whether you're a military veteran. You can skip both.

As you use your dashboard

  • Roadmap tasks you check off, the route you pick, items you save, First Shift Kit checks, and certifications and expiration dates you add.

If you join the pilot email list

  • Your name, email, ZIP and career stage, sent to us through a Google Form.

If you email us

  • Whatever you choose to include in your message.

What we don't collect

  • No payment or bank information. The pilot is free.
  • No Social Security numbers, driver's license numbers, medical information or test scores.
  • No precise location, no advertising IDs, and no analytics or tracking pixels.

How We Use It

  • To build your dashboard and roadmap: the classes, jobs, test dates and funding that fit you.
  • To keep your dashboard in sync across your devices when you're logged in.
  • If you joined the pilot list, to email you about new classes, testing dates and scholarships near you. Every email includes an unsubscribe link and our mailing address.
  • To answer your questions and fix listing errors you report.

We don't sell or rent your personal information, we don't share it for advertising, and we don't use it to make automated decisions about you.

Where It's Stored and Who Helps Us

Your dashboard is saved in your own browser. If you have an account, a copy is also saved with your account so it follows you to other devices. These companies help us run the site and only handle data to provide their service:

We may share information if the law requires it, or to protect the safety of a person. If Ladder Up is ever sold or merged, your information would move with it under this same policy, and we'd tell you first.

Children and Teens

Ladder Up is for people 13 and older. We don't knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you're under 13, please don't create an account or join the list. If we learn that a child under 13 has given us information, we will delete it.

Users 13 to 17 must have a parent's or guardian's permission to use Ladder Up, and we ask them to confirm it. A parent or guardian can ask us to see or delete a teen's information at any time by emailing jack@ladder-up.net.

How Long We Keep It

  • Account and dashboard data: until you delete it or ask us to delete your account.
  • Pilot email list: until you unsubscribe, ask us to remove you, or the pilot ends.
  • Emails you send us: as long as needed to answer you.

When you ask us to delete your data, we do it within 30 days.

Your Choices

  • See or change your answers at any time from your dashboard with "Edit My Answers."
  • Unsubscribe from pilot emails with the link in any email, or by emailing us.
  • Delete your data with the "Delete My Data" link at the bottom of any page. It clears your dashboard from your browser and your account right away. To remove your login and any pilot sign-up too, send the deletion email it offers, or email jack@ladder-up.net.
  • Get a copy of the information we have about you by emailing us.

Security

The site uses HTTPS encryption, passwords are handled by our login provider and never stored in plain text, and we limit who can see sign-up data. No system is perfectly secure. If a breach ever affects your personal information, we will notify you as required by Illinois law.

Cookies and Browser Storage

See our Cookie Policy. In short, we only use storage the site needs to work: your dashboard and, if you log in, your login session.

Changes to This Policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top. If a change affects how we use information you've already given us, we'll tell you by email or on the site before it takes effect.
