    Class Finder

    EMT, Paramedic and fire academy programs, plus CPAT test sites, sorted by distance from your ZIP. Costs are the program's own published numbers. Where a program doesn't publish one, we say so.

    Program

    Pass rates: none of these programs publish NREMT first-attempt pass rates on their program pages, so Ladder Up doesn't rank by them yet. Always confirm cost and dates with the program before you enroll.

    Continuing Education

    For firefighters already on a department: OSFM certification courses and specialty training, from Advanced Technician Firefighter and Engineer to technical rescue, water rescue, hazmat and officer classes. Includes every scheduled IFSI class in Illinois. Upcoming sessions are listed first.

      Job Board

      Open full-time and part-time firefighter postings, soonest deadline first. Part-time and paid-on-call jobs are a common first fire job once you have your EMT license and Basic Operations Firefighter. Every listing links straight to its application and to the posting we pulled it from.
