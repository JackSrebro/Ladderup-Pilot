Cookie Policy

Effective November 9, 2026

Ladder Up doesn't use advertising cookies, analytics or tracking pixels. It only stores what the site needs to work: your dashboard, and your login if you have an account.

What We Store

NameTypeWhyHow long
lu:* (for example lu:profile, lu:pathDone, lu:saved, lu:zip)Browser storage (localStorage)Saves your questionnaire answers, roadmap progress, saved items and ZIP on your device so your dashboard works.Until you log out, delete your data or clear your browser

Netlify login session (for example gotrue.user and nf_jwt)Browser storage and cookieKeeps you logged in when you have an account.Until you log out

All of these are strictly necessary for features you ask for, so we don't ask for consent to them. We don't use them to track you across other sites.

Outside Services

When your browser loads the site, it also requests fonts from Google Fonts and code libraries from Cloudflare (cdnjs), unpkg and jsDelivr. Like any website request, these services receive your IP address and browser details. Ladder Up doesn't set cookies for them. If you choose Google sign-in or open our Google sign-up form, Google's own cookies and privacy policy apply.

If We Ever Add Analytics

If we add analytics or anything that isn't strictly necessary, we'll update this policy first and ask for your consent before it runs.

Your Choices

  • Use "Delete My Data" at the bottom of any page to clear everything Ladder Up stores.
  • You can also clear site data in your browser settings. This logs you out and resets your dashboard on that device. If you have an account, your dashboard comes back when you log in again.
