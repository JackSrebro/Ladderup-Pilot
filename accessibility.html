Accessibility

Effective November 9, 2026

We want Ladder Up to work for everyone, including people who use screen readers, keyboards, magnification or voice control.

Our Goal

We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1, Level AA.

What We've Done

  • Every part of the site works with a keyboard, including tabs, the questionnaire, the roadmap checklist and the flip cards.
  • Pages use proper headings, labels and landmarks for screen readers.
  • Text and buttons meet color contrast standards in light and dark mode.
  • Icons that carry no information are hidden from screen readers, and meaningful images have text alternatives.
  • Animations turn off when your device is set to reduce motion.
  • The site works at phone sizes and when you zoom to 200%.

Known Limits

Some links go to outside sites we don't control, such as department applications, school registration pages, testing companies, PDFs and our Google sign-up form. Their accessibility may vary.

Tell Us About a Problem

If something is hard to use, email jack@ladder-up.net with the page and what happened. We'll reply within 5 business days and can send the information you need in another format.
